📚 Study notes

Quick revision tips for your Grade 7 learning.

📖 English

Parts of speech: Nouns name people, places or things. Verbs show actions. Adjectives describe nouns. Adverbs often describe verbs.

Remember: Check subject–verb agreement and punctuation.

🔬 Science

Scientific method: Observe, ask a question, make a prediction, investigate, record results and draw a conclusion.

Remember: Keep investigations fair by changing one factor at a time.

➗ Mathematics

Problem solving: Read carefully, identify the information, choose an operation, calculate and check your answer.

Remember: Show your working clearly and include units when needed.

🌍 Geography

Map skills: Use the title, key, scale and direction. A map key explains symbols and a scale compares map distance with real distance.

🏛️ Civic Education

Good citizenship: Respect others, follow fair rules, protect public property and take part in community activities.

💚 Health

Healthy habits: Eat a variety of foods, stay active in a balanced way, get enough sleep and practise personal hygiene.

💻 ICT

Digital safety: Keep passwords private, ask a trusted adult before sharing personal details and check information before trusting it.
